# Old riddles, the kind a sphinx would ask. Format: docs/riddles.md.
theme: Riddle of the Sphinx

Q: How many hounds guard this gate?
A: 2
A: two

Q: What walks on four legs in the morning,
Q: two legs at noon, and three in the evening?
A: man
A: human
A: people
H: The morning, noon and evening of a life.

Q: The more of me you take, the more you leave behind.
Q: What am I?
A: footsteps
A: steps
A: footprints

Q: The one who makes it sells it. The one who buys it never uses it.
Q: The one who uses it never knows.
A: coffin
A: sarcophagus
A: tomb
H: Every pharaoh ordered one.

Q: I have cities but no houses, rivers but no water,
Q: and roads that no one walks. What am I?
A: map
H: Press M.

Q: What has keys but opens no locks?
A: piano
A: keyboard

Q: What gets wetter the more it dries?
A: towel

Q: What has a neck but no head?
A: bottle
A: flask

Q: Wrapped in linen, I wait for eternity. What am I?
A: mummy

Q: Speak my name and I am gone. What am I?
A: silence

Q: What has hands but cannot clap?
A: clock
A: watch

Q: What is always coming but never arrives?
A: tomorrow

Q: You must break me before you can use me.
A: egg

Q: The more there is of me, the less you see.
A: darkness
A: dark

Q: What can run but never walks, has a mouth but never talks,
Q: and a bed but never sleeps? The pharaohs lived beside the longest one.
A: river
A: nile

Q: What has one eye but cannot see?
A: needle
A: storm

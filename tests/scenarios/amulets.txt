# Amulets (docs/plan/amulets.draft.md): one worn at a time, swapped freely; strength and armour add, the health
# amulet keeps the HP's share of the max, the save keeps the worn one; trap warding cuts spike damage (grand: none);
# regeneration heals 1 HP a second while no monster chases the player and no poison runs; poison warding wards off
# some poisonings. The Amulets tab and the HUD slot.
# Amulet ids (give amulet N): 0-3 strength, 4-7 armour, 8-11 health, 12-15 poison, 16-19 traps, 32-33 regeneration.
seed 1
level tests/levels/empty_row
wait 10
expect worn == -1
give amulet 3
give amulet 0
expect might == 0
wear grand_amulet_of_strength
expect worn == 3
expect might == 6
wear lesser_amulet_of_strength
expect worn == 0
expect might == 1
wear off
expect worn == -1
expect might == 0
give amulet 6
wear amulet_of_armor
expect armor == 4

# health: the HP keeps its share of the max, put on and taken off
give amulet 11
expect maxhp == 50
hurt 25
wear grand_amulet_of_health
expect maxhp == 65
expect hp == 33
wear off
expect maxhp == 50
expect hp == 25
wear grand_amulet_of_health
savegame amulets.sav
wear off
loadgame amulets.sav
expect worn == 11
expect maxhp == 65
expect hp == 33

# the tab and the HUD
give amulet 13
give amulet 13

screenshot inventory_amulets
select amulet_of_regeneration
wait 5
screenshot inventory_regeneration
key i

screenshot hud_amulet

# regeneration: 1 HP a second while safe
wear amulet_of_regeneration
expect safe == 1
expect maxhp == 50
expect hp == 25
wait 2s
expect hp == 27
# poisoned: none (the poison drains instead)
poison weak
wait 2s
expect hp < 27

# trap warding: without, 30 HP lost in a second; grand, no spike damage; lesser, about a quarter off (22)
level tests/levels/spikes_one
wait 5
wear off
walk right 3.5
wait 1s
expect hp == 20
level tests/levels/spikes_one
wait 5
give amulet 19
wear grand_amulet_of_trap_warding
walk right 3.5
wait 1s
expect hp == 50
level tests/levels/spikes_one
wait 5
give amulet 16
wear lesser_amulet_of_trap_warding
walk right 3.5
wait 1s
expect hp == 28

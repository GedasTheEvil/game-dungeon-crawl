# The monsters' field notes (plan texts-to-content, docs/texts.md): the journal's description, in the archaeologist's
# words, written after the first kill. [monster.<label>], the label of its row in src/world/monster_kinds.cpp.

[monster.scarab]
note: A beetle the size of my fist. Its bite stings.

[monster.worm]
note: Slow as sand, but its jaws shut tight and hold. Whoever calls it Shai-Hulud gets no answer.

[monster.plant]
note: It cannot move. Never stand next to it. Never touch it: hitting it counts too.

[monster.anubis]
note: A statue of Anubis that forgot to stay on its stand. Traps do not stop him, and his stone shrugs off fire.

[monster.rat]
note: Quick, filthy and always hungry.

[monster.giant-rat]
note: A rat as big as a dog. Jumps at me over the pits.

[monster.bat]
note: Flies down out of the dark. Hard to hit in the air.

[monster.giant-bat]
note: Its wings are wider than my arms.

[monster.mimic]
note: Not every chest in this tomb holds treasure. Ask Osiris.

[monster.giant-scarab]
note: A shell like a shield, and it jumps.

[monster.boss-scarab]
note: The mother of the scarabs. Her young come when she calls.

[monster.vampire-bat]
note: Grown fat on blood. Every bite makes it stronger.

[monster.mummy]
note: Wrapped in bandages and patient. It waited three thousand years for me.

[monster.anubis-boss]
note: The guardian of the last tomb. His statues step out of the stone when he calls.

[monster.crocodile]
note: Sobek's own. In the water I cannot outrun it.

[monster.scorpion]
note: Pale as straw, quick on its eight legs. The sting burns for a long while.

[monster.cobra]
note: The uraeus, the cobra on the pharaoh's crown, alive. It spits before it bites, and both burn.

[monster.giant-cobra]
note: Black as the night of Apep, long as a boat. It spits farther than I can jump.

[monster.giant-scorpion]
note: Dark as old blood and as big as a dog. Its sting does not wear off quickly.

[monster.egg-cluster]
note: Leathery eggs glued in resin. Something moves inside. Smash them before they hatch.

[monster.scorpion-queen]
note: Serket herself, or her daughter. Her sting burns like fire, and her eggs are everywhere.

[monster.apep]
note: The serpent of chaos, who swallows the sun each night. The sand is his road.

[monster.sobek]
note: The lord of the Nile, green as the river. He charges like a flood; let the wall stop him.

[monster.lotus]
note: Pretty, blue, and it wants my leg. One bite and I could sleep for a week. Odysseus's men ate the lotus; this
	one eats Odysseus's men.

[monster.mummy-priest]
note: He read the curses at the funerals. He still knows them all. The dead rise at his word.

[monster.camel-spider]
note: Ten legs, jaws like shears, and faster than I can run. The soldiers' stories say it chases your shadow. Not
	venomous, they say. It does not need to be. It spins no webs. So who made these?

[monster.hippo]
note: Taweret's people. On the walls they protect mothers. In the water they protect nothing.

[monster.ushabti]
note: A servant figure, buried to answer for the dead when the gods call them to work. It says 'here I am' on its
	chest. It means it.

[monster.cursed-mimic]
note: Some chests bite. Some bite the mind. The dark ones with the purple glow in the lock: leave them shut.

[monster.seth]
note: Seth in the shape of the red beast. Horus took him with a harpoon. I have a spear; it will have to do.

[monster.baboon]
note: Thoth's baboons, the priests said. Thoth was a scribe. These throw things.

[monster.rerek]
note: The Book of the Dead has a spell for driving back Rerek, the serpent of the underworld. I have it by heart
	now. He has not read it.

[monster.mother-of-webs]
note: Found the one who makes the webs. She does not hide from the torches; she puts them out.

[monster.babi]
note: The heart-eater. I keep mine, thank you.

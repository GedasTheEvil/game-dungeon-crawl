# Amulet of venom: poison monsters on hit

Status: draft 2026-10-07. Split off [amulets.md](amulets.md); for later.

## Idea

* An amulet (tiered like the others) that gives the player's hits a chance to poison the monster: lesser 10%,
  grand 30% (minor and normal in between, open).
* **New:** monsters cannot be poisoned today; poison only runs on the player ([poison-and-antidote](solved/poison-and-antidote.md)).

## Open

* Minor and normal chances.
* Monster poison: damage over time per tier, how long, does it stack; immune monsters (the poisoners themselves,
  mummies, the egg cluster?). Journal note on a creature's poison resistance.
* Shown how: a green tint or bubbles on the monster, its health bar.
* Name: "venom" to tell it from the poison resistance amulet.

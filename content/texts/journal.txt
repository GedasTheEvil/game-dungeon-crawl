# The journal's own words (plan texts-to-content, docs/texts.md): the field notes (FieldNote, fieldNoteText in
# src/world/journal_text.cpp), one [note.<slug>] each in FieldNote order, then the diary pages (DiaryPage). The notes
# say the rules as the game has them (PlayerStats, ItemBag, the mechanisms): keep them in step.

[note.health]
title: Hit points
text: Every bite, blow and trap costs me health: the red bar in the corner. When it runs out, the expedition is
	over. A new level fills it up.

[note.levels]
title: Experience
text: Every creature I kill and every riddle I answer teaches me something. With enough of it I reach a new level:
	more health and more stamina, both filled up at once, and any poison gone. Every fifth level my skin gets tougher,
	every eighth my arm stronger.

[note.stamina]
title: Stamina
text: Jumping and running (Shift) tire me out: the yellow bar. Each jump uses up some of it, and running drains it
	all the time. When I walk or stand, it comes back by itself.

[note.keys]
title: Keys and gates
text: A key opens every gate of its colour on this level. Some gates answer to a lever of their colour instead. The
	gate of a boss's lair stays shut until the boss is dead.

[note.poison]
title: Poison
text: Some stings and bites poison me: the health bar turns green and keeps draining after the fight, armour or not,
	and it can kill me. The same poison again only starts over; a stronger or weaker one runs alongside it. A new level
	clears all poison.

[note.weapons]
title: Weapons
text: Copies of my weapons are in chests, and now and then a creature leaves one; with enough copies a weapon can be
	upgraded in the inventory (U). The old club gains the most from it. 1 takes up the next hand weapon, 2 the next one
	to shoot or throw. Every creature takes each kind of blow differently: I note it down.

[note.blunt]
title: Smash
text: A club smashes, and so do the mace, the sling's stones and the throwing stick. They hit hard, but they do not
	cut.

[note.slash]
title: Cut
text: A blade cuts: the bronze sword, the khopesh (a curved sword), the epsilon axe. Long cuts, quick to follow one
	another.

[note.pierce]
title: Stab
text: A point stabs: the flint knife, the spear, the javelin, the duckbill axe, every arrow. It finds the gaps
	between scales and plates.

[note.health-potions]
title: Health potions
text: Bitter herbs from the marshes heal my wounds, the priests' brew twice as much. When I am in a hurry, H drinks
	the one that fits best.

[note.stamina-potions]
title: Stamina potions
text: Date wine, or the red beer of Hathor: a stamina potion gives me my stamina back. When I am in a hurry, 0
	drinks the one that fits best.

[note.might]
title: Blood of Montu
text: It tingles. Whatever is in it, my arm hits harder after I drink it, forever.

[note.armor]
title: Granite of Ptah
text: Ptah's granite: my skin becomes hard as a temple wall, and every blow hurts a little less, forever.

[note.life]
title: Breath of Osiris
text: The breath of Osiris: more health forever, and all my wounds close at once.

[note.antidote]
title: Milk of Renenutet
text: The milk of Renenutet cures every poison in me. = drinks one, but only while I am poisoned.

[note.dart-traps]
title: Dart traps
text: A slab with a cobra on it sank under my foot, and darts shot out of three holes in the wall above it, tipped
	with venom. A rat is too light to press it, a mummy is not, and the darts hit whoever is in their way. A jump over
	the slab sets nothing off; a jump at the right moment lets them pass under me.

[note.resistance]
title: Resistance potions
text: Cobra venom, a drop at a time. For two minutes a poisoned bite or sting often does nothing to me: half the
	time after the lesser potion, nearly always after the greater one. The greater potion also cures a poison already
	in me. A poison guard amulet adds its share.

[note.heka]
title: Heka
text: The guardian's heka, the magic the gods hold, went into me. It tastes of river mud and old incense. Each
	guardian gives one; on the Heka page (K) I spend it on a skill, forever. There are more skills than guardians down
	here, so I choose with care.

[note.serkets-favour]
title: Serket's favour
text: Serket likes me. Her children still bite, but they hurt me less: every poison on me lasts half as long, with
	the same damage each second.

[note.sharp-eye]
title: Sharp eye
text: Horus lent me an eye. I will give it back. Probably. Meanwhile my bows, the sling, the javelin and the
	throwing stick all reach a third farther: even a foe far away is in my sights.

[note.curse-weakness]
title: Weakness
text: A curse sits on my arm: half my might is gone, and the greater curse takes all of it and more. Nothing I drink
	removes a curse. Two minutes, then it fades. Another curse of the same kind restarts the two minutes.

[note.curse-clumsiness]
title: Clumsiness
text: Cursed with clumsy hands: every blow, shot and throw does a fifth less damage, and half as much under the
	greater curse. My venom is not affected. Two minutes and it passes; no potion helps.

[note.curse-vulnerability]
title: Vulnerability
text: Cursed: my armour feels like papyrus. It stops only two fifths of what it did. Two minutes, and nothing cures
	it. I should not get hit in that time.

[note.curse-fatigue]
title: Fatigue
text: A curse drained me: my stamina fell to a fifth and does not come back by itself for two minutes. A stamina
	potion still works.

[note.curse-disease]
title: Disease
text: A cursed sickness: my body holds less health, a third less, much less under the greater curse. It gives the
	health back when it passes, in two minutes. No antidote helps.

[note.curse-slowness]
title: Slowness
text: Cursed legs: I walk and climb at half speed, and I run hardly faster than I used to walk. Two minutes. Traps
	made for a quick step are dangerous for me now.

[note.curse-palsy]
title: Palsy
text: My hands shake from a curse: no bow, sling or throw finds its mark, so I do not try. My melee weapons still
	work. Two minutes.

[note.curse-insanity]
title: Insanity
text: My mind is clouded. The club stays in my hand, and I cannot use my map, journal, pack or potions. Two minutes,
	if I live.

[note.fire]
title: Fire
text: Fire sets what it hits on fire: it keeps burning for a few seconds after the hit. Armour does not stop it,
	only a fire guard amulet does. Water puts it out, and in the water a flame barely scorches. A brazier lights my
	next five arrows (E by it). Mummies, it turns out, burn very well. Whatever shrugs off an arrow, a bat's thin wing,
	a mimic's dry wood, a plant, a mummy, burns instead: fire arrows are my answer to it. Scorpions are used to the
	heat.

[note.favour-of-hapi]
title: Favour of Hapi
text: The water parts for me now. Not much, but enough to stop wading like a heron.

[note.blessing-of-isis]
title: Blessing of Isis
text: Isis has a knot for every curse. She ties fast.

[note.water-blessing]
title: Blessing of Hapi
text: Hapi's jar. For two minutes the Nile and I are on good terms: I wade at full speed, sprint and jump in half
	water as if it were a street. With his favour it lasts twice as long and the water carries me.

[note.canopic-jars]
title: Canopic jars
text: Four jars, four sons of Horus: one each for the liver, the lungs, the stomach and the guts. Somebody swapped
	the insides for treasure. I try not to wonder where the insides went.

[note.jumped-bite]
title: Jumping a bite
text: It snapped at my heels and caught only air. A small one bites low: jump as it opens its jaws and the bite goes
	under me. Over a tall one a jump does not help.

[note.sand]
title: Sand
text: Sand. Underground. Somebody left a door open, three thousand years ago. I wade through the drifts at three
	quarters of my pace, and running in it is out of the question. Jumping still works.

[note.quicksand]
title: Quicksand
text: The sand swallowed my boots, then my shins. Standing still makes it worse: the longer I stay, the slower I
	get, until I am stuck and it squeezes. A jump gets me out, if I have the breath for it. Up close it swirls, slowly.
	Look before you step.

[note.sun-mirrors]
title: Sun mirrors
text: The priests knew how to bring the sun underground. So did a certain archaeologist with a staff. A shaft of
	daylight runs straight until rock stops it; a bronze mirror sends it on the way its pointer shows, and I can turn
	one a quarter at a time. A sun disc, an Aten, that catches the light opens the gates of its colour for good. The
	dead do not like the sun: it sets them alight.

[note.trapped-chests]
title: Trapped chests
text: Some chests bite back. Darts from the lock, a puff of green gas, a curse on the lid, deeper down a nest of
	scarabs. Up close a trapped one looks darker, as if it knew. Half of them were robbed long ago and only the trap is
	left; the rest hold a little more than a plain chest. The Eye of Horus sees most traps before they go off.

[note.tomb-curses]
title: Tomb curses
text: The lid said 'cursed be he who opens this'. I opened it. The lid was right. The dead write their threats on
	whatever they leave behind: lids, jars, the little servant figures. Not every one means it, and the curse runs out,
	as any curse.

# The hour notes (plan campaign-texts): written on entering a zone, one per ZoneId in order.
[note.hour-1]
title: The First Hour
text: The gate of the tomb. Ra's boat enters the Duat and the first gate closes behind it. It closed behind me too,
	and I did not even have a boat. The floors have teeth. Mind the floors.

[note.hour-2]
title: The Second Hour
text: Bats, and a smell of old leather. The wall says the second hour is the water of the blessed, where the grain
	grows. Nothing grows here except my doubts. And the bats.

[note.hour-3]
title: The Third Hour
text: The dark thickens, and the walls are hung with webs. The third hour is the hour of the judging of Osiris. I am
	being judged by spiders. I keep a torch close.

[note.hour-4]
title: The Fourth Hour
text: The priests' quarter. Ra's boat has to be towed over sand here, says the wall, and the priests wrote a great
	deal on it. I can read it at last. I almost wish I could not.

[note.hour-5]
title: The Fifth Hour
text: The cavern of Sokar's secret, the wall says, and in my private chapter: the cavern of Serqet's scorpions. I do
	not like her sense of humour. I count my antidotes.

[note.hour-6]
title: The Sixth Hour
text: The midnight of the journey: Ra and Osiris meet in the dark, and the serpents come to watch. The wall says:
	the dead are made new here. I will settle for staying the same.

[note.hour-7]
title: The Seventh Hour
text: Apep waits here, says the wall. I wish the wall were wrong. The seventh hour is the hour of the great serpent
	and of water: the boat is hauled through the cisterns. So am I.

[note.hour-8]
title: The Eighth Hour
text: Sand again, red and endless. Seth stands in the prow of the boat and drives the serpent back with his spear.
	The wall says the dead here are clothed. I would settle for a hat.

[note.hour-9]
title: The Ninth Hour
text: The land of Sokar, where the ground gives way and the worms sleep. The ninth hour feeds the dead with grain,
	the wall says. I would be glad of a sandwich. I would be gladder of a floor.

[note.hour-10]
title: The Tenth Hour
text: A lake of fire, and baboons on its shore. In the tenth hour the lake is cooled for the boat of Ra, so there
	must be water somewhere. I look for it first. Everything else second.

[note.hour-11]
title: The Eleventh Hour
text: The necropolis. Every trap I have met, in one place, and the road to the last gate. The eleventh hour is the
	one before the dawn. The dawn is not what I would call close.

[note.hour-12]
title: The Twelfth Hour
text: The hall of the scales, the last hour. Ra is born again at its far end, says the wall. I have a feeling that
	something here will weigh my heart. I did bring it. Let me be light.

# The amulet notes (plan amulet-extras): one per AmuletType in order, [note.<amulet model>], the numbers
# of the four tiers.
[note.amulet-strength]
title: Jackal tooth
text: A jackal's tooth on a cord. Worn, it adds to every blow I strike: Might +1, +2, +4, +6, from the lesser amulet
	to the grand one. One amulet at a time.

[note.amulet-armor]
title: Bronze scarab
text: A bronze scarab. Worn, it adds to my armour, which takes a share of every hit I suffer: 1, 2, 4 or 6 from
	the lesser to the grand one.

[note.amulet-health]
title: Carnelian heart
text: A carnelian heart, and it beats with mine: my health is greater by 5, 10, 20 or 30 percent while I wear it,
	from the lesser to the grand one. Taking it off does not hurt me.

[note.amulet-poison]
title: Serket's scorpion
text: Serket's scorpion. Her children spare me: a poisoned bite or sting fails 10, 25, 50 or 80 percent of the time,
	lesser to grand. It adds to a resistance potion's share.

[note.amulet-traps]
title: Eye of Horus
text: The eye of Horus sees the spikes first. Spike, death trap and dart damage is cut by 25, 50, 75 percent, and
	the grand eye makes me immune. A chest's trap goes off as much less often.

[note.amulet-blunt]
title: Djed pillar
text: The djed pillar does not bend. Worn, smashing blows hurt me 8, 16, 28 or 40 percent less, lesser to grand.
	Falling rocks too, I hope.

[note.amulet-slash]
title: Knot of Isis
text: The knot of Isis: blades slip on it. Cuts hurt me 8, 16, 28 or 40 percent less, from the lesser to the grand
	knot.

[note.amulet-pierce]
title: Shen ring
text: The shen ring: points glance off it. Stabs hurt me 8, 16, 28 or 40 percent less, from the lesser to the grand
	ring. Darts and arrows too.

[note.amulet-regeneration]
title: Faience lotus
text: A faience lotus. It opens when all is calm: while nothing chases me and no poison runs, it heals me, 1 health
	a second, 2 with the grand one. There is no lesser or minor lotus.

[note.amulet-venom]
title: Wadjet's cobra
text: Wadjet's rearing cobra, and my blade grows fangs: 10, 15, 20 or 30 percent of my hits poison the foe, weakly,
	weakly, then worse, then strongly. What resists poison resists this. A foe the poison kills still counts as mine.

[note.amulet-fire]
title: Sun disc
text: Ra's disc burns daily. Fire does not care about my armour, but this does: fire hurts me 20, 40, 60 or 80
	percent less, lesser to grand. Never nothing at all.

[note.amulet-crushing]
title: Seth's mace
text: The mace head of Seth: what it hits stays hit. Worn, my smashing blows deal 10, 20, 30 or 50 percent more,
	lesser to grand. Only the smashing part counts: a mace gains all of it, a club nearly all.

[note.amulet-cleaving]
title: Montu's khopesh
text: The sickle sword of Montu parts what it meets. Worn, my cutting blows deal 10, 20, 30 or 50 percent more,
	lesser to grand. Only the cutting part counts: a sword gains most of it, a club hardly any.

[note.amulet-piercing]
title: Neith's arrowhead
text: The arrowhead of Neith finds the gap. Worn, my stabbing blows deal 10, 20, 30 or 50 percent more, lesser to
	grand. Only the stabbing part counts: every arrow gains all of it.

[note.stun]
title: Stun
text: A good knock on the head and the beast forgets what it was doing: it stands there for a moment and a half,
	stars round its head, looking for the rest of its thoughts. A bat even forgets how to fly. A club or a mace does
	it best (a sling stone too), a sword or a spear never; the gods' great beasts shake it off twice as fast, and not
	twice in a row. Rude, but it works.

[note.rings]
title: The rings
text: A round plate in the floor, rings of bronze hidden in the ceiling over it. I stood on it and pressed: five rings
	came down round me, one after the other, a flash of white, and they rose again in another hall. Another plate,
	the same rings above. It goes both ways. At last, a ring for my Rings pocket. It does not fit on a finger.

[note.stargate]
title: The ring of seven marks
text: A ring of stone taller than two men against the wall, a track of signs inside it, seven marks round its rim. I
	put my hand on it and the track turned: a mark lit with a clunk, then the next, six of them. The seventh flickered
	and went dark. Chevron seven... will not lock. Seven chevrons. Only six symbols on the wall. Daniel would know.

[note.dial-device]
title: The dial
text: A stone lectern before the ring, two circles of signs round a red dome. I pressed the dome. Nothing. I pressed
	it again, harder. Indeed. Whatever it was built to call does not answer, and Abydos is not on this map.

# The prologue (plan journal-intro): how the archaeologist came here, a title and up to three paragraphs (p1..p3)
# per page. The statue is Anubis (the boss of the last level), the ankh the one won there; the club is the
# starting weapon (items.cpp).
[diary.arrival]
title: Saqqara, the third week
p1: The dig gave us nothing for nineteen days. On the twentieth, under the sand of the east trench, a statue no
	catalogue knows: a jackal-headed man, life-sized, an ankh in his hand, and no name on the base. The crew would not
	go near it. I am a scientist. I touched the ankh.
p2: Light. Then cold stone and darkness. No sand, no sky, no crew. One torch burns on the wall, as if someone
	expected me.

[diary.descent]
title: Later
p1: At my feet lay an old acacia club. Not a trowel, but it will do: something scratches in the dark down the hall.
	The corridor slopes down. There is no way back that I can see, so down it is.
p2: The first signs I can read speak of one thing only: the ankh, life without end. Someone built this maze to keep
	it. Or to keep someone in. If there is a way out, it is at the bottom, with the ankh.
p3: I will keep notes. Someone should know what happened here.

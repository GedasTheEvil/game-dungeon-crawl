# Resistance potion and amulets

Status: draft 2026-10-06. Split off [poison-and-antidote.md](poison-and-antidote.md): the antidote only
cures, it gives no protection afterwards.

## Idea

* A new potion that gives resistance to poison for a while (less poison damage, or none).
* Amulets with special resistances: worn, they protect for as long as they are on (poison, perhaps a damage type,
  [solved/damage-types-and-resistances.md](solved/damage-types-and-resistances.md)).

## Open

* Resistance: a share off the poison damage, a shorter poison, or immunity? How long the potion lasts.
* Amulets: a new equipment slot in the inventory, one amulet at a time? Which resistances, found where.
* Models and icons for both.

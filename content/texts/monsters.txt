# The monsters' field notes (plan texts-to-content, docs/texts.md): the journal's description, in the archaeologist's
# words, written after the first kill. [monster.<label>], the label of its row in src/level/monster_kinds.cpp.

[monster.scarab]
note: A black beetle the size of my fist. It bit me. I did not know beetles could bite.

[monster.worm]
note: The sand moved, and then the sand had teeth. Whoever calls it Shai-Hulud gets no answer. I keep to the stones
	now.

[monster.plant]
note: A thorn bush, growing in a tomb that never saw the sun. I brushed past it and my arm still itches. I walk round
	it now.

[monster.anubis]
note: A statue of Anubis that forgot to stay on its stand. I heard the stone grind behind me. I will never trust a
	statue again.

[monster.rat]
note: Rats. Of course there are rats. Filthy, hungry, and not the least bit afraid of me.

[monster.giant-rat]
note: A rat as big as a dog, and it looked at me like dinner. I have met rats before; never one like this. Ugh.

[monster.bat]
note: Something brushed my hair in the dark. Then it came back. I hate bats.

[monster.giant-bat]
note: A bat with wings wider than my arms. Its shriek went right through my head. I had to sit down afterwards.

[monster.mimic]
note: Not every chest in this tomb holds treasure. Ask Osiris. I reached for the lid, and the lid reached for me.

[monster.giant-scarab]
note: This beetle is bigger than any beetle I know. It even tried to throw a ball of dung at me. Ew!

[monster.boss-scarab]
note: Khepri, who rolls the sun across the sky and made himself. Every beetle in the room came when he called. I did
	not stay to count them.

[monster.vampire-bat]
note: The bat of Shezmu, the god of the wine press and of blood. It drank from me and looked the better for it. I did
	not.

[monster.mummy]
note: Wrapped in bandages and patient. It waited three thousand years for me. I wish it had waited a little longer.

[monster.anubis-boss]
note: Anubis himself, the one who weighs the hearts. His statues stepped out of the walls to help him. My heart was
	beating too fast to weigh.

[monster.crocodile]
note: A log in the water. Then the log opened its mouth. Sobek's own. I am not swimming here again.

[monster.scorpion]
note: Pale as straw and quick on its eight legs. It got me on the ankle. I shake out my boots every morning now.

[monster.cobra]
note: The uraeus, the cobra on the pharaoh's crown, alive. It spat in my face. I am still wiping my eyes.

[monster.giant-cobra]
note: Black as the night of Apep, long as a boat; no cobra I know comes near it. I did not wait to see how far it
	could spit.

[monster.giant-scorpion]
note: Dark as old blood, as big as a dog, and bigger than any scorpion I want to meet. It caught me in its claws and
	held on. I screamed, I admit it.

[monster.egg-cluster]
note: Leathery eggs glued in resin. Something moved inside. I smashed them all and did not look closely.

[monster.scorpion-queen]
note: Serket herself, or her daughter. Her eggs were everywhere, and they hatched while I watched. My skin still
	crawls.

[monster.apep]
note: Apep, the serpent of chaos, who would swallow the sun and fails every night. He sank into the floor and came up
	behind me. I lit every torch I had.

[monster.sobek]
note: Sobek, lord of the waters, green as the river. He came at me like a flood, and the wall stopped him before I
	could. His crocodiles were bad enough.

[monster.lotus]
note: Pretty, blue, and it wanted my leg. One bite and I could have slept for a week. Odysseus's men ate the lotus;
	this one eats Odysseus's men.

[monster.mummy-priest]
note: The High Priest of Anubis. He read the curses at the funerals, and he still knows them all. The dead got up when
	he spoke. I wanted to lie down.

[monster.camel-spider]
note: The soldiers' stories say it chases your shadow. They are right, and it runs faster than I do. It spins no webs,
	they say. So who made these?

[monster.hippo]
note: Taweret's people. On the walls they protect mothers. This one came at me like a boat with teeth.

[monster.ushabti]
note: A servant figure, buried to answer for the dead when the gods call them to work. It says 'here I am' on its
	chest. It meant it.

[monster.cursed-mimic]
note: Some chests bite. This dark one, with the purple glow in the lock, bit my mind. My head is still not right.
	Leave them shut.

[monster.mimic-jar]
note: Seth had a whole household. This jar kept the beer cold and the fangs sharp. The next sealed jar I break from
	arm's length.

[monster.seth]
note: Seth in the shape of the red beast, the biggest hippo I ever hope to see. Horus took him with a harpoon. I had a
	spear; it had to do.

[monster.baboon]
note: Thoth's baboons, the priests said. Thoth was a scribe. These threw hot coals at me, so I threw things back.

[monster.rerek]
note: The Book of the Dead has a spell for driving back Rerek, the serpent of the underworld. I have it by heart now.
	He has not read it.

[monster.mother-of-webs]
note: Found the one who makes the webs, bigger than any spider I care to remember. She put my torches out, one after
	the other. I have never loved a lamp so much.

[monster.babi]
note: Babi, the bull of the baboons, who lives on the entrails of the dead. The loudest of them. I keep my insides,
	thank you.

[monster.tiny-spider]
note: The camel spider's young, or its smaller cousin; nobody in the camp could tell me which. Never one alone: they
	come out of the cracks in threes and fours. I stamped and stamped.

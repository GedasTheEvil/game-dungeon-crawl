# The details on a skill's card of the Heka page, under its effect (plan texts-to-content, docs/texts.md).
# [skill.<name>], the name of its row in src/level/rules/skills.cpp. rank2: the details of rank II (plan skill-upgrades).

[skill.stun]
lore: A good knock on the head stops anything for a moment, gods' beasts too.
rank2: Rank II: a stunned foe learns nothing. Your blows stun nearly twice as often; a guardian still shakes it off sooner.

[skill.words-of-thoth]
lore: Thoth, lord of writing, opens the carved words on the walls.

[skill.dodge]
lore: Step aside at the last moment: a bite or a sting may find only air.
rank2: Rank II: you read the blow before it starts. About one in five misses.

[skill.power-hit]
lore: Put your back into it: hold the blow, and the longer, the harder it lands. Paid in stamina.
rank2: Rank II: the whole body behind the blow. Held blows land 75% harder than rank I's.

[skill.eye-of-horus]
lore: Horus lends an eye: bows, sling, javelin and throwing stick carry farther, and only a foe on top of you is too
	close to shoot.
rank2: Rank II: the healed eye sees all. Reach 60% farther, and nothing is too close to shoot.

[skill.favour-of-hapi]
lore: Hapi, the Nile in flood, lets you through his shallows.

[skill.serkets-favour]
lore: Serket's children still bite, but they do not mean it as much.
rank2: Rank II: Serket calls her children off. Poisons last a third as long, about one in five never lands.

[skill.blessing-of-isis]
lore: Isis has a knot for every curse. She ties fast.
rank2: Rank II: her knots hold tighter. Curses last a third as long, about one in five never lands.

[skill.flaming-arrows]
lore: Sekhmet lends her flame: the arrow flies on fire, and what it hits burns.
rank2: Rank II: Sekhmet's breath. Twice the fire on each arrow, for the same stamina.

[skill.ka-of-osiris]
lore: Osiris came back from death. A share of his ka keeps you standing longer.
rank2: Rank II: half again of his ka. Max health +50%.

[skill.strength-of-montu]
lore: Montu, the falcon lord of war, lends you his arm.
rank2: Rank II: his other arm too. Might +10.

[skill.djed-of-ptah]
lore: Ptah's djed pillar, the backbone of Osiris, stiffens your skin.
rank2: Rank II: a second pillar. Armor +10.

[skill.breath-of-shu]
lore: Shu, the air between earth and sky, fills your lungs.
rank2: Rank II: the whole sky in your chest. Max stamina +50%.

[skill.speed-of-mafdet]
lore: Mafdet, the cheetah who runs down snakes, lends her feet. Not in water or sand; a jump reaches as far as ever.
rank2: Rank II: she runs beside you. Walk and sprint 35% faster.

[skill.wisdom-of-seshat]
lore: Seshat measured every temple: riddle hints at once, the royal name, sealed rooms on the map.

[skill.disarm-trap]
lore: Containers' traps fail 4 times in 5, trapped ones and mimics glow red up close, none is empty.

[skill.quiver-of-neith]
lore: Neith, the huntress of the crossed arrows, refills your quiver before you reach for it.
rank2: Rank II: she hunts with you. 70% more shots, back 44% faster.

[skill.bleed]
lore: A clean cut keeps opening after the blade is gone. Blood and bone, not linen or stone.
rank2: Rank II: the cut finds the vein. Foes bleed nearly twice as often.

[skill.arrows-of-satet]
lore: Satet, she who shoots, whets the head: bow shots cut 5 deeper, and a hit that lives may keep bleeding.
	Not the sling, the stick or the javelin.
rank2: Rank II: her own arrowheads. Bow shots cut 10 deeper, and the wound opens at medium.

[skill.sting-of-serket]
lore: Serket lends her sting: now and then a bow shot that does not kill leaves medium poison in the wound.
rank2: Rank II: the sting goes deep. The poison is strong.

[skill.fang-of-wadjet]
lore: Wadjet, the spitting cobra on the brow, wets your blade: now and then a blow that does not kill leaves medium
	poison.
rank2: Rank II: the fang bites. The poison is strong.

[skill.touch-of-imhotep]
lore: Imhotep, the builder who became a god of healing, lays a hand on you: 1 health every 5 seconds, while nothing has
	hurt you for 5 seconds and no fire, poison or bleeding runs. A chasing foe does not stop it. It adds to the lotus
	amulet and the garnet rings.
rank2: Rank II: the whole hand. 0.25% of your max health every second, more as your health grows (1 health in 4
	seconds at 100).

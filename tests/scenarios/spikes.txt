# Spike damage (Dungeon::updateTraps): one hit every 100 ms while the player stands in a spike tile, growing by 1
# every 3 hits (1 1 1 2 2 2 3 3 3 4 ... HP), reset once they step out for 200 ms. Two spike tiles side by side hurt
# at the same rate as one. No damage while the inventory is open (the world is paused).
# Levels: tests/levels/spikes_one, spikes_two.
seed 1

# the tile edge (x 4.0) is outside the hitbox (0.32 tiles round the centre); the centre hurts
level tests/levels/spikes_one
wait 5
walk right 3
wait 1s
expect hp == 50
walk right 0.5
expect hp == 45
wait 1s
expect hp == 15

# paused by the inventory, then hurt again, the streak reset
level tests/levels/spikes_one
wait 5
walk right 3.5
key i
wait 1s
expect hp == 47
key i
wait 500ms
expect hp == 40

# two spike tiles: one hit per 100 ms, never two; the 0.36 tiles between their hitboxes reset the streak
level tests/levels/spikes_two
wait 5
walk right 3.5
wait 500ms
walk right 1
wait 500ms
expect x > 5.4
expect hp == 9

# The earlier expedition's notebook (plan expedition-notes, docs/texts.md): page n is the Page tile of value n
# (content/levels). The archaeologist who came before, D. J. (a nod to Daniel Jackson): a linguist, confident at first,
# then doubts; the last page warns before the fork. Each page lies a level or so before what it hints at; the hints are
# the game's rules (monster_kinds.cpp, the levels): keep them in step.

[page.1]
heading: Day 1
text: Touched the jackal statue. Should not have touched the jackal statue. No crew, no sky. I still have my
	notebook, two pencils and my glasses. If anyone finds this: I am D. J., a linguist, not a soldier.

[page.2]
heading: Day 2
text: The beetles grow bigger the deeper I go. A blade skids off the shell; a point slips between the plates.
	Something very large clicks down below. I named it Ramses. It does not like the name.

[page.3]
heading: Day 3
text: A chest bit me today. Not a figure of speech. Wood cracks under a club; my knife only stuck in the lid. New
	rule: knock before opening.

[page.4]
heading: Day 5
text: Ramses is dead. I did not enjoy it. Behind him a key and a way down, never a way up. Every door here opens one
	way: deeper. Someone planned this.

[page.5]
heading: Day 6
text: Water! I could have kissed it, until the log in it opened its eyes. The crocodiles swim much faster than I
	wade. Stay out of the pools when you can, or cross them fast.

[page.6]
heading: Day 8
text: Bats. A club swats them, an arrow goes through the wing. Something up in the roost drinks blood and heals with
	every bite. Do not let it bite. Thin out its friends first.

[page.7]
heading: Day 9
text: Webs across the halls, thick as sailcloth, and things that spin them: eight legs, fast, and they hate fire. A
	torch in the hand keeps them at a distance. Mine went out once. I will not describe that hour.

[page.8]
heading: Day 11
text: The dead do not stay down here. Linen tears under a blade and burns like paper: dip the arrows in a brazier
	and they go up. Jack would have brought a flamethrower. Who is Jack?

[page.9]
heading: Day 12
text: A hall with two shafts side by side, and a priest's warning on the wall. I took the short one; the long one
	had water in it and I am a bad swimmer. Both come out in the same hall. There is no way back up to the other.

[page.10]
heading: Day 13
text: I can read most of the walls now. The priests write of a ring of stone with seven marks, a road between
	worlds. If it is down here, it is hidden behind a riddle. My last pencil to see it.

[page.11]
heading: Day 15
text: Scorpions everywhere, and eggs. Something lays them. Cut or burn the clusters before her brood hatches, and
	carry an antidote. Two. I sneezed for an hour: allergic to something here. Everything.

[page.12]
heading: Day 16
text: The roof weeps green in the nest halls. One drop burns, two put me on my knees. Wait for the gap between the
	drops, then run. The antidote tastes of boiled socks. I have drunk four.

[page.13]
heading: Day 18
text: The serpent temple. The cobras rear up when I come close and spit from afar: jump the spit. The great one
	below dives into the floor and comes up behind you. Turn around. Always turn around.

[page.14]
heading: Day 20
text: My pencil is half its length. I have stopped believing in a way up. I still believe in a way out. Not the same
	thing, I know. It is what I have.

[page.15]
heading: Day 23
text: Darts fly from the wall where the floor clicks: jump the plate. Lost my glasses for an hour and found them on
	a mummy. It was wearing them. We had words.

[page.16]
heading: Day 24
text: The cisterns have a deep end. Down there I swim, and swimming tires me twice as fast as walking. Rest on dry
	stone, drink the blessing of Hapi before the water, not in it. Crocodiles in every pool, hippos in the big ones.

[page.17]
heading: Day 26
text: Sand. Underground! It drags at the feet, and where it swirls it swallows: jump out, do not wait. The spiders
	here hate the light. I sleep by the torches. Well, I lie by them.

[page.18]
heading: Day 29
text: Sunlight through a crack, the first in weeks. Bronze mirrors turn it onto the sun discs that open the doors,
	and the mummies burn in it. The red one in the pool below charges, then roars. Stand aside.

[page.19]
heading: Day 30
text: Worms. Not the kind in the garden. They hear a footstep on sand and come up under it; the spiders hunt in the
	dark between. A fork ahead: one way over loose drifts, one along the rock. Rock, rock, rock.

[page.20]
heading: Day 31
text: Some doors want a name, not a key. The king's name is carved in a cartouche on the same level: find it, copy
	it, speak it. Names had power here. Mine does not seem to.

[page.21]
heading: Day 35
text: The chests are darker down here, and some of them follow you. One bit me and my mind went cloudy for minutes.
	Dark wood: knock with a club first. Abydos is not on this map.

[page.22]
heading: Day 38
text: A lake of fire, and baboons on its shore who throw coals from afar. Corner them or run them down; never stand
	in the open. The water is the one cool place here. A second fork ahead: the right is shorter. It always is.

[page.23]
heading: Day 40
text: The necropolis. Loose ceilings everywhere: when the stone rumbles, keep walking. The jackal from the statue is
	on every wall. He waits at the bottom. I think he has always been waiting.

[page.24]
heading: The last page
text: If you read this, take the left gate. I took the right. Behind it, the tomb of the one who came before me, and
	worse. The ankh is real. Tell them I was right. D. J.

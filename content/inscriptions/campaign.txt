# The campaign's wall inscriptions, by level (plan hieroglyph-cartouches). Format: docs/inscriptions.md.
# Real texts in short translations, a few hints for the level they stand in. After changing a G: or style: line,
# rebuild the atlas: python3 tools/textures/inscriptions.py

# ---- level 6

id: 1
title: The offering formula
style: stela
G: M23 X1 R4 X8 Q1 D4 A40 O3 X3 W22 F1 G54
T: An offering which the king gives to Osiris, lord of Abydos:
T: bread and beer, oxen and fowl, linen and alabaster,
T: every good and pure thing on which a god lives,
T: for the spirit of the one buried here.
N: The standard tomb greeting. The ancient "Dear Sir or Madam".

# ---- level 7: the first crocodile waits in the flooded stretch to the west.

id: 2
title: The curse of Meni
style: column
G: I3 N35A I14 N25 A2
T: The crocodile be against him in the water,
T: the snake be against him on land,
T: he who would do a thing against this tomb.
T: Never did I do a thing against him.
T: It is the god who will judge him.
N: A crocodile in the water. Oddly specific. Noted.

# ---- level 8

id: 3
title: The ushabti spell
style: column

T: O ushabti, if I am called to do any work
T: in the realm of the dead, to till the fields,

# ---- level 9

# ---- level 10: the vampire bat (Bat of Shezmu) heals by part of the damage it deals.

# ---- level 11: the first mummies.

# ---- level 12: red key, green lever, gold key, in that order.

id: 8
title: The Book of Gates
style: row
G: O32 O32 O32 I14 R8 Z2
T: Every hour of the night has its gate, every gate its keeper.
T: The carnelian door opens first, then the turquoise,
T: and the amber door last of all.
T: Who knows the order passes.
N: Red, then green, then gold. Even the ancients had keycards.
